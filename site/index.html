OUR APPROACH

Less noise.
More substance.

Aldercrest Reserve is a concept for an independent, California-based precious-metals and collectible-coin shop. The idea is simple: make collecting feel more considered.

01

Start with the specifics.

Weight, composition, condition, and clear images. The details should inform your decision, not leave you guessing.

02

Choose with intention.

A focused selection instead of endless listings. Room for everyday bullion and pieces with a little more character.

03

Keep it straightforward.

Understand what you're buying and how it will reach you. No promises about future returns. Just the piece in front of you.

 PROPOSED OPERATING STANDARDS

Trust is in the details.

These are proposed requirements for the future store, not claims about an operating dealer or the sample inventory.

  1. Document the source.Retain supplier invoices and item-level acquisition records.
  2. Verify the actual piece.Use an appropriate authentication process. A grading number alone is not a complete check.
  3. Describe it accurately.Use actual photos for individual collectibles and verified specifications, conditions, and availability.
  4. Define the delivery process.Confirm insurance, fulfillment, identity checks, and applicable return terms before accepting an order.
